Reservation Guidelines & Dress Code

Selected Slot: 2 Guests · 2026-11-20 Dinner 19:30 · Harbour View Window

1. Deposit & Cancellation Policy:
To secure your reservation and seasonal ingredients, a deposit is required (fully deductible from your final bill). Any cancellation or modification must be made at least 24 hours prior to your reservation time; otherwise, the deposit will be forfeited.

2. Table Holding Time:
Your table will be held for 15 minutes from the reserved time. Please contact our concierge if you anticipate arriving late.

3. Dress Code:
Our dress code is Smart Casual. Gentlemen are kindly requested to refrain from wearing sleeveless shirts, athletic shorts, or open-toed sandals.

4. Corkage & Cake Cutting Fee:
Corkage is HK$ 400 per 750ml bottle of wine/champagne. A cakeage fee of HK$ 250 applies for outside celebration cakes.

5. Dietary Restrictions:
Please indicate any food allergies or dietary requirements in the "Special Requests" box on the next page.
